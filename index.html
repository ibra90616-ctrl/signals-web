Not investment advice. These ratings are a mechanical vote across ~26 indicators applied to past price on a single timeframe. They carry no information about positioning or your own risk tolerance and time horizon — and they change every candle. Indicators agreeing is not independent confirmation: they are correlated formulas reading the same price series, so fourteen moving averages "agreeing" is closer to one observation than fourteen. The calendar is shown precisely because the indicators are blind to it. Use this to see market state, not to decide trades.
