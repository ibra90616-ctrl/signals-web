Not investment advice. These ratings are a mechanical vote across ~26 indicators applied to past price on a single timeframe. They carry no information about monetary policy, economic releases, positioning, or your own risk tolerance and time horizon — and they change every candle. Indicators agreeing is not independent confirmation: they are correlated formulas reading the same price series, so fourteen moving averages "agreeing" is closer to one observation than fourteen. Use this to see market state, not to decide trades.
